How a piece is made

From one feeling to a finished frame

  1. i.

    Feeling

    Every piece starts from an emotion, a myth or a song, written down before any image exists.

  2. ii.

    Light

    Lighting is designed like a real set: key, fill, rim and the colour of the shadows.

  3. iii.

    Character

    Casting, wardrobe and posture define who the figure is and how it holds the space.

  4. iv.

    Finish

    Upscaled, retouched and graded by hand, delivered print-ready.

The studio
The camera taught me light. AI lets me build the world it falls on.

LL·ONE is the studio of a fashion photographer working with artificial intelligence. Years of sets, models and real light shape every generated image, so each one is directed, not just prompted. The work moves between classical painting, Nordic stillness and a silver-toned future.
